How the score works

Total = w₁·Skills + w₂·Semantic + w₃·Experience + w₄·Education

  • Skills – required skills found (exact = 100%, related skill such as “Spring” for “Spring Boot” = 50%).
  • Semantic – cosine similarity of TF-IDF vectors where synonyms (GCP = Google Cloud Platform) share one dimension.
  • Experience – non-overlapping years from dated roles vs. the requirement.
  • Education – highest degree vs. the minimum in the posting.

Scores support a recruiter's decision; they should not be the only basis for rejecting a candidate.
